# Google VRP Scope
## In Scope
- *.google.com
- *.blogger.com
- *.youtube.com
- *.gstatic.com
- Google Android apps on Google Play
- Google Chrome extensions published by Google

## Out of Scope
- Third-party apps on Google Play
- Websites not owned by Google
- Social engineering, phishing

## Rewards
- XSS: $100 - $15,000
- Open redirect: $100 - $5,000
- CSRF: $100 - $10,000
- RCE: $75,000 - $101,010

## Quick Targets
1. Search params on google.com
2. Redirect URLs in Google services
3. CSRF on Google account settings
